About

A builder of capability, with one foot in government and one in the university.

I spent a decade at the University of Ottawa's Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining Lab learning how machines find the right service among thousands. Back in Riyadh I put that discipline to work on a harder discovery problem: finding, growing and certifying the people a national AI strategy depends on.

The through-line is the same everywhere — at a regulator designing its innovation model, at the Digital Government Authority writing the standards every agency is measured against, and at SDAIA planning how a million citizens learn AI: build the standard first, then the programme, so that what you build outlasts you.
