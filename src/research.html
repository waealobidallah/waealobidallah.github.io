Research

I work at the meeting point of information systems and applied AI: how organisations adopt and govern emerging technology, and how machine learning can be made trustworthy and useful in healthcare, industry and public services. The research grew out of a decade at the University of Ottawa's Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining Lab and now runs alongside national capability-building work at SDAIA.

Research interests

Six threads.

AI capability & workforce development

Competency frameworks, skills assessment and certification, national talent pipelines — the research behind the AI Badges and capability strategy.

Digital transformation & innovation management

Maturity models, innovation systems (ISO 56002), technology-adoption theory (TAM/UTAUT, SEM) in government and industry.

Applied machine learning in healthcare & industry

Deep learning for fault diagnosis and medical imaging, resource-constrained deployment, explainability.

IoT security & risk assessment

Hesitant-fuzzy and multi-criteria models for assessing the security of medical and transportation IoT systems.

Blockchain adoption & data ownership

Adoption drivers across industries; blockchain-based personal health records and Web3 identity.

Web services discovery & NLP

Word embeddings and clustering for service discovery and recommendation — the PhD line of work, now extended to generative AI in education.

Prospective MSc students

Want to write your thesis with me?

I supervise MSc theses in Information Systems at IMSIU. I look for students who want to solve a real problem with data — in government, healthcare, industry or education — and are willing to publish. Pick a topic or propose your own in the same spirit, then email me a one-page idea and your transcript.
